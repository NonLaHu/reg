Department of Information Science & Technology

Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.)

Information Science and Technology (IST)
Duration: 6 Years
Key Requirements: Internship, 3 Field Trips, Mini Thesis

This six-year undergraduate program leads to a Bachelor of Engineering in Information Science and Technology (B.E. IST). The curriculum emphasizes practical skills through group projects and key programming languages like Java, J2EE, Python, and MATLAB. To graduate, students must complete three field trips (after the third and fourth years), a minimum two-month internship, and a mini-thesis in their final semester.

Field Areas: The program covers five major fields: Information Science, Software Development, Networking & Security, Computer Vision, and Digital & Embedded Systems.

Master of Engineering (M.E.)

Information Science and Technology (IST)
Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: B.E. in IT, CEIT, or IST

This is a two-year postgraduate program consisting of one year of coursework and one year for a master's thesis. Admission requires a relevant Bachelor's degree and passing a master's entrance exam and oral test. After completing the coursework, students have the opportunity to join the Double Degree Program (DDP) to earn a second Master's degree from Miyazaki University, Japan.

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.)

Information Technology (IT)
Duration: 3 Years
Eligibility: Relevant M.E. or M.C.Sc/M.C.Tech

Our Ph.D. in Information Technology is a three-year program structured with one year of coursework and two years dedicated to doctoral research. This program is heavily research-oriented, preparing students for careers in advanced research and academia. Prospective students must hold a relevant Master's degree and pass both an entrance exam and an oral test to be admitted.
