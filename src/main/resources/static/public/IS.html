Introduction

The Department of Information Science at the University of Technology (Yatanarpon Cyber City) is a collaborative community of motivated students, competent lecturers, and enthusiastic researchers. We are dedicated to educating students to become talented specialists in their fields and to conducting innovative scientific research.

We train our students to master 21st-century skills, including collaboration, teamwork, creativity, imagination, critical thinking, and problem-solving. Upon graduation, students possess strong work-readiness skills and an in-depth knowledge of information science. This enables them to find sophisticated solutions to the complex issues that arise within our society.

Our Vision

To become a hub for intellectual professionals, practitioners, and future leaders in the field of emerging information technologies.

Our Mission

  • To train students to become innovators with the highest standard of theoretical and practical knowledge in emerging information technology.
  • To empower students to solve challenging real-world problems through the effective use of the most innovative technology.
  • To provide a wide-access learning environment enhanced by the application of ICT.
"To Build a Better Society Through ICT"
— Department Motto
