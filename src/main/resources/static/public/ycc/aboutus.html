ISO 9001:2015 Certification

ISO 9001:2015 is being implemented in a number of technological universities in Myanmar, including our university (UTYCC). Our University has been implementing this OMS system since 2019 to improve our interactions, in accordance with the requirements of the international Standard and intended to fill the need and expectations of relevant interested parties.

Re-Certification Achieved: June 6-7, 2022

Why we implement ISO 9001:2015 in our university is to enable the accreditation system to be implemented to achieve international status. It is one of the basic step functions for us to get accreditation.

Our commitment to quality management ensures that we consistently provide educational services that meet customer and regulatory requirements while enhancing student satisfaction.
