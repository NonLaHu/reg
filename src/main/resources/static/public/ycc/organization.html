Global Ranking Update (July 2025)

According to Webometrics’ July 2025 edition, UTYCC’s global ranking is 30,523, with a country rank of 57. The university’s openness rank is 9,001, and its excellence rank is around 7,422. Academic Collaborations & Institutional Focus

UTYCC remains active in research and international collaboration. It implemented work-based learning initiatives and focused efforts on e-learning adoption, notably being the first to establish an e-Learning Center in Myanmar through an ASEAN–Korea Cyber University partnership, starting July 11, 2012
