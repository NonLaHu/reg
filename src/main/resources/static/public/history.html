University of Technology (Yatanarpon Cyber City)

History

The University of Technology (Yatanarpon Cyber City), also known as UT-YCC, is recognized as one of Myanmar's premier higher education institutions. It is located near Pyin Oo Lwin, alongside the Mandalay-Lashio Road within Yatanarpon Cyber City. The university was established in October 2010 under the Ministry of Science and Technology and has rapidly become a center of technology education.

UT-YCC offers five specialized Bachelor of Engineering degrees: Information Science and Technology, Computer Engineering, Electronics, Mechanical Precision and Automation, and Materials and Metallurgy. Each program generally spans six years. In addition to its undergraduate offerings, the university provides Master's and Doctoral degree programs.

The university's mission focuses on fostering a collaborative learning environment that brings together faculty, students, and stakeholders to utilize technology-enhanced learning for personal and regional development.

UT-YCC pioneered e-learning in Myanmar, establishing the country's first E-Learning Center on July 11, 2012. This was part of the ASEAN-KOREA Cyber University (ACU) initiative, aiming to develop and share digital educational content among ASEAN universities. UT-YCC remains an active member of this project.

Committed to quality, UT-YCC has implemented a Quality Management System to pursue international accreditation. The university collaborates internationally through a double degree program with Miyazaki University in Japan and works with the Korea International Cooperation Agency to enhance e-learning solutions.

Prospective students are encouraged to join UT-YCC to benefit from excellent academic programs, prepare for rewarding careers, and contribute to technological advancement locally and globally.
