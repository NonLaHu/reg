General Information

When was the UTYCC founded?

University of Technology (Yatanarpon Cyber City) was established on 17 October 2010, under Myanmar's Ministry of Science and Technology

How many students are there in the University as of now?

According to Edurank, UTYCC's enrollment is listed at approximately 12,000 students.

Admissions

What are the requirements for the applicants?

Admission Requirements at UTYCC

    For First-Year (Undergraduate) Applicants

  • A minimum score of 280 or above in four subjects: English, Mathematics, Chemistry, and Physics
  • A total score of 420 or above across six subjects.

    For Transfers (Second Year to Final Year Applicants) Required documents include:

  • NRC (National Registration Card)
  • Passport-style photo
  • Household copy
  • COVID-19 vaccination certificate
  • NRC copy of parents or guardians.

Academic Programs

How long does it take to complete a Graduate Program?

Master of Engineering (M.E.) Programs:

  • Duration: 2 years
  • 1st year → Coursework (advanced classes in the chosen specialization)
  • 2nd year → Research and Master's Thesis

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Program (for reference):

  • Duration: 3 years
  • 1st year → Coursework
  • 2nd-3rd years → Research & Dissertation
What graduate programs does UTYCC offer?

UTYCC offers a wide range of graduate programs including:

  • Ph.D. in Computer Engineering
  • Ph.D. in Computer Science
  • Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering
  • Ph.D. in Information Technology
  • Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering
  • Ph.D. in Material Science and Engineering
  • Ph.D. in Biotechnology
