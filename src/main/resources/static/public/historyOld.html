About UTYCC

The University of Technology (Yatanarpon Cyber City), also known as UT-YCC, is one of Myanmar's premier universities. Established in October 2010 under the Ministry of Science and Technology, the university is conveniently located near Pyin Oo Lwin, alongside the Mandalay-Lashio Road.

Academic Programs

UT-YCC offers five specialized Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) programs, each requiring five years of dedicated study:

  • Information Science and Technology
  • Computer Engineering
  • Electronics Engineering
  • Mechanical Precision
  • Advanced Materials Engineering

In addition to our undergraduate degrees, we also provide comprehensive Master's and Doctoral programs to further advanced learning and research.

Pioneering E-Learning

A pioneer in digital education, UT-YCC established the first E-Learning Center in Myanmar. On July 11, 2012, this center became part of the ASEAN-KOREA Cyber University (ACU) project, developing and sharing online content with other universities. UT-YCC is a proud member institute of the ACU project.

International Collaboration

To achieve international standards and provide global opportunities, the university actively collaborates with international partners. This includes a double-degree program with Miyazaki University in Japan and an ongoing e-Learning project with the Korea International Cooperation Agency (KOICA).

Our Commitment to Quality

As part of our commitment to excellence, UT-YCC has implemented the ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System (QMS). Since 2019, this system has helped us standardize our processes and improve our educational services to meet the needs and expectations of our students, faculty, and stakeholders.

Implementing the ISO 9001:2015 standard is a foundational step in our journey toward international accreditation, ensuring our operations align with global best practices. The university successfully completed its ISO 9001:2015 Re-Certification audit on June 7, 2022, reaffirming our dedication to continuous improvement.
